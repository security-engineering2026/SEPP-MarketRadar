# Market Radar — Per-Row Research Gate Standard

Every remaining-work row must carry a research packet before PASS.

Minimum: 10 non-GitHub sources + 5–10 comparable software/products.

Required packet:
1. source list and URLs;
2. source type (official docs, standards, vendor/product, independent analysis, community where appropriate);
3. access date;
4. claim/pattern extracted;
5. contradiction/uncertainty;
6. comparable product list;
7. observed workflow/architecture/UX/reliability pattern;
8. adopted/rejected/adapted decision;
9. exact repository requirement/module/test affected;
10. implementation and test consequence.

Research may not be satisfied by ten pages repeating the same source. Prefer independent sources and primary documentation.

The packet is evidence of engineering research, not evidence that the product itself works. Product PASS still requires executable proof in the correct environment.
